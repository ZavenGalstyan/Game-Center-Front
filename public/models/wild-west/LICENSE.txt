Wild West: Card Showdown — 3D assets

All files in this folder are CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
They were re-packed for the web (glTF-Transform: meshopt geometry, WebP
textures resized to 1024 px; "props-lite" = ~25% triangles + 256 px textures
for the Low preset); the content is otherwise unchanged.

props/*.glb, props-lite/*.glb — Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/models), CC0:
  painted_wooden_chair_02, wooden_stool_01, Lantern_01, lantern_chandelier_01,
  Chandelier_01, wine_bottles_01, brass_goblets, mantel_clock_01,
  vintage_grandfather_clock_01, wine_barrel_01, wooden_crate_02, bull_head,
  fancy_picture_frame_01, ornate_mirror_01, round_wooden_table_01,
  CashRegister_01, vintage_cabinet_01, vintage_pocket_watch, Rockingchair_01,
  brass_candleholders, wooden_bucket_01, quiver_tree_01, namaqualand_boulder_02
  (brass_candleholders, quiver_tree_01 and namaqualand_boulder_02 are
  simplified to ~35% triangles in the full set too)

tex/*_d|_n|_arm.webp — Poly Haven textures (https://polyhaven.com/textures), CC0:
  old_wood_floor, wood_floor_worn, wood_plank_wall, raw_plank_wall,
  wooden_panels, decrepit_wallpaper, clay_plaster, weathered_plank_siding,
  dry_ground_01, wood_table_worn, lacquered_cherry_wood, brown_leather,
  velour_velvet, wood_cabinet_worn_long
  (_d = diffuse, _n = OpenGL normal, _arm = AO/roughness/metalness)

sky/*.hdr — Poly Haven HDRIs (1k), CC0, used for image-based lighting:
  goegap (day), kiara_8_sunset (sunset), dikhololo_night (night)
sky/*.jpg — the same HDRIs' tonemapped panoramas from Poly Haven, resized to
  4096×2048, seen through the saloon windows and doors.

Shared with other games (not in this folder):
  ../neon-mercenary/male.glb, female.glb, anims.glb, m_dark.jpg, f_dark.jpg and
  ../mma/anims.glb — Quaternius "Universal Base Characters" + "Universal
  Animation Library", CC0. The cowboys' clothing, hats, moustaches, belts,
  badges and the poker table, cards, chips, walls, bar, windows, doors, piano
  and street buildings are generated in code by the game — no other
  third-party assets. Card faces, chip faces, signs and posters are drawn at
  runtime on canvases (original artwork). All sound is synthesised at runtime.
